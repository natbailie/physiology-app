Practice at this bedside

Cool, tachycardic trauma patient. Pressures below. Which shock state?

Explanation (shown after answering in the app)

Both filling pressures are empty and the resistance is up — there is simply not enough blood in the circuit. Note the low mixed venous saturation: extraction is intact, unlike the septic pattern. Compensation hides the severity until it fails, which is why a normal pressure must never reassure you here.

Lessons

Why compensation lies to you

A young patient can hold a normal blood pressure while the cardiac index collapses — the baroreflex raises resistance, rate and tone together until it is exhausted. Hypotension in haemorrhage is therefore a late sign. Related modules: Venous Return — the curves behind these filling pressures; Cardiac Cycle — the pressure-volume consequences of a failing ventricle.
