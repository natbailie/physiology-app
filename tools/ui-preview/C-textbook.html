PLATE 12 · CHAPTER IV · EXAMINABLE — UKMLA / MRCP

Shock: haemorrhage and the venous return curve

Lower stressed volume → lower mean systemic filling pressure → lower venous return. The heart can only pump what it receives. Predict (Exercise 12.3) before running the plate.

APPENDIX A · FORMULAE   ·   MATERIA MEDICA (UK TOP-100)

What to carry onto the ward

A.3 — CORRECTED CALCIUM

Cacorr = Cameas + 0.02 × (40 − albumin)

Worked: 2.05 + 0.02×(40−28) = 2.29 mmol/L (ref. 2.2–2.6). See Plate 31, calcium handling.

A.7 — MEAN ARTERIAL PRESSURE

MAP ≈ ⅓ SBP + ⅔ DBP = 65 mmHg

M.14 — ACE INHIBITORS (—pril)

Ramipril, enalapril, lisinopril. Lower angiotensin II and aldosterone; first-line in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction. See Plate 12 — watch afterload fall and renal perfusion change.

M.22 — LOOP DIURETICS

Furosemide, bumetanide. Block NKCC2 in the thick ascending limb. See Plate 24.
