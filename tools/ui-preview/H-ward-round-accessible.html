Practice

Cool, tachycardic trauma patient. Pressures below. Which shock state?

Explanation (shown after answering in the app)

Both filling pressures are empty and the resistance is up — there is simply not enough blood in the circuit. Note the low mixed venous saturation: extraction is intact, unlike the septic pattern. Compensation hides the severity until it fails, which is why a normal pressure must never reassure you here.

Practice

Margaret's creatinine is rising on a diuretic. The team ask whether to stop it. What is the kidney doing?

Explanation

Output is down and everything follows from that one fact. The kidney reads low flow as low volume and turns RAAS on; retaining more volume loads a ventricle that could not cope with the last litre. The creatinine is a consequence, not a second diagnosis — the loop breaks by treating the pump.

What changed for university use, and what deliberately did not

  • Text contrast: light --faint #7c8da6 → #5a6f88, dark faint raised to #9db4cc; secondary sizes 10–11px → 12px. Borders #dfe6ef/#1d3352 → #687d95/#5a7a9e for the 3:1 non-text floor.
  • Opacity removed from text: nav, theme buttons and roadmap cards no longer dim via opacity; “soon” is dashed border + Roadmap tag.
  • Acuity: word + icon + bordered solid tag in both themes (▲ Crash, ● Due, ✓ Check, ○ New, ℞ prescription). No translucent washes; referrals carry an ◔ icon and underlined link.
  • Vitals/competency glow kept as decoration: values stay large mono; meaning now also carried by words (! Shock), solid/dashed/dotted map strokes, and a text caption. prefers-contrast:more drops the glow.
  • Meters: 8px + border + visible 38% · 3 due text + progressbar roles. Trends gain details-table alternatives.
  • Focus: new :focus-visible 3px outline + offset on every link, button, chip, slider and radio. Skip link added; landmarks, one h1 per demo, labelled sliders, fieldset quizzes.
  • Unchanged: ward-round metaphor, dark default, cyan brand, layout, real beds/routes/panels, no XP/leaderboards/sounds/streak punishment/expiry/paywalled reference.

Port notes if greenlit: apply the H token deltas to index.css neutrals and chips only; signals already held by palette.test.ts. Still required outside this preview: publish the accessibility statement, keyboard + screen-reader audit at beta, and keep fictional-patient / not-a-clinical-tool disclosures on every page.
