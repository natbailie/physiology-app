Model parameter. Moving it changes the diagram and every readout.

Practice

Cool, tachycardic trauma patient. Pressures below. Which shock state?

Explanation (shown after answering in the app)

Both filling pressures are empty and the resistance is up — there is simply not enough blood in the circuit. Note the low mixed venous saturation: extraction is intact, unlike the septic pattern. Compensation hides the severity until it fails, which is why a normal pressure must never reassure you here.

Lessons

Compensation hides haemorrhage: pressure holds while output collapses. Related: Venous Return — the curves behind these filling pressures; Cardiac Cycle — pressure-volume consequences of a failing ventricle.

Practice

Margaret's creatinine is rising on a diuretic. The team ask whether to stop it. What is the kidney doing?

Explanation

Output is down and everything follows from that one fact. The kidney reads low flow as low volume and turns RAAS on; retaining more volume loads a ventricle that could not cope with the last litre. The creatinine is a consequence, not a second diagnosis — the loop breaks by treating the pump.

Compliance notes (what changed from concept F, and why)

What had to change

  • Light default. Concept F opens dark with glowing vitals. Both UK systems are light-first; dark here is an enhancement behind the second switcher, with identical content and contrast floors.
  • Focus you can see. Thin blue outline replaced with a thick yellow block plus dark edge on every interactive element, including the phone-style bed list.
  • Acuity is word + tag. CRASH / DUE / CHECK / NEW tags are bold, bordered, and pass 4.5:1 as text. Removing colour still leaves the word — WCAG 1.4.1.
  • Vitals become definition lists. Glowing numerals become <dl> panels with tabular numerals and text alternative for every meter and sparkline (details-table pattern).
  • Structure a screen reader can use: skip link, one h1 per service page, ordered headings, landmarks, labelled sliders with values, quiz as fieldset + radios, back links, unique titles, lang="en-GB".
  • Headers done honestly. NHS variant uses the NHS header pattern. GOV.UK-style variant uses an own-service black header — current guidance says services outside the GOV.UK proposition must not use the crown header/footer. Do not ship the GOV.UK variant with GOV.UK branding.
  • Kept from the app deliberately: no XP/coins, no leaderboards, no sounds, no streak punishment, no expiring prescriptions, reference never paywalled, British spelling, reduced-motion respected.

Data bindings (repo-verbatim)

  • RoundBoard → bed list (Amina A04, George A07, Ruth B02, Nadia C01, Brian C05, Colin C09, Margaret D03, Derek D08). Acuity illustrative; app derives it.
  • StudyStrip → summary list; StudyReport → prescribed rows; DISCIPLINES → subject grid; ModulePage slots → diagram / readouts / controls / charts / quiz / explainer.
  • SHOCK_PANEL / ABG_PANEL / CARDIORENAL_PANEL → observation rows above. Values illustrative of shape.

Before shipping this style

  • Publish an accessibility statement and test with keyboard + at least two screen-reader/browser combinations; run an audit at beta.
  • Confirm font licensing (GDS Transport / Frutiger) or document the Arial fallback; confirm button/tag hexes against the live design-system release.
  • Keep fictional-patient and not-a-clinical-tool disclosures on every page, as above.
